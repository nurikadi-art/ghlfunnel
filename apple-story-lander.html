Chapter 1: The Breaking Point

Let me tell you about the night everything changed.

It was 2am. I was still at my desk. My eyes burned from staring at spreadsheets. I had 10 staff members, a business doing okay on paper, and a to-do list that never ended. I was working 15-hour days, 6 days a week.

My wife walked in. We hadn't really talked in weeks — not really talked. Just logistics. Who's picking up the kids. What bills are due. That kind of thing.

"You're never here. Even when you're home, you're not here. The kids are growing up and you're missing it."

We had a fight. Not the yelling kind. The quiet kind. The kind where you realize you're losing the things that actually matter while chasing the things that don't.

She went to bed. I sat there in the dark, staring at nothing.

The next morning, I called my dad.

He's been my mentor my whole life. Not in a "business guru" way. In a real way. He's the one who taught me that working hard is only half the equation. Working smart is the other half.

I told him everything. The business. The stress. The fight with my wife. How I felt like I was falling behind while doing more than ever.

He listened. Then he said something I'll never forget:

"Son, the world changed. You didn't change with it. Stop working harder. Start working different."

He was right. I had been so busy in the business, I couldn't see what was happening around me.
